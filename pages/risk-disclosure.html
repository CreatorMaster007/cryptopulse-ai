UNDERSTAND THE RISKS

Risk Disclosure

High volatility

Cryptocurrency prices can rise or fall sharply, including within short periods. Past performance does not predict future results.

Loss of capital

You may lose some or all of the money you commit. Never invest money you cannot afford to lose and consider your own financial circumstances.

Technology and custody

Smart-contract bugs, exchange failures, hacks, lost credentials, network congestion and irreversible transfers can cause permanent losses.

Liquidity and market integrity

Low liquidity, slippage, manipulation, wash trading and misleading token information can affect prices and execution.

Regulatory and tax uncertainty

Rules and tax treatment vary by jurisdiction and can change. Seek independent professional guidance.

Limits of CryptoPulse AI tools

Our indicators and calculators are educational aids only. A token scanner may miss risks; a high score is not a safety guarantee. No tool predicts returns or removes investment risk.
