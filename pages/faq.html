Frequently Asked Questions

Is CryptoPulse AI an exchange or broker?

No. The website is an informational and educational dashboard. It does not execute trades or hold customer funds.

Is the market data real time?

Some data is retrieved from third-party providers and may be delayed, rate-limited, unavailable or subject to provider updates. Check the source and timestamp shown on the page.

Does the Market Advisor give buy or sell signals?

No. Its rule-based commentary is educational and should not be treated as a personal recommendation or prediction.

Does a safe token scan guarantee a token is safe?

No. A scan checks selected warning indicators only. It is not a smart-contract audit and cannot establish that a token is safe.

Can I rely on the calculators for exact returns?

No. Results are estimates and may exclude taxes, slippage, funding, network charges and exchange-specific fees.

How can I report an issue?

Use the Contact page and include the relevant URL and details. Do not send passwords, private keys or seed phrases.
