Crypto Formula Library

Core formulas used in market and risk analysis.

Return on Investment (ROI)((Sell value − Cost) ÷ Cost) × 100

Measures profit or loss relative to initial investment.

Market CapitalizationPrice × Circulating Supply

Approximate market value of circulating tokens.

Fully Diluted Valuation (FDV)Price × Max / Total Supply

Hypothetical valuation if all stated supply were circulating.

Average Buy PriceTotal amount spent ÷ Total coins

Weighted average cost across multiple purchases.

Liquidity / Market CapPool liquidity ÷ Market cap × 100

A rough comparison; pool depth and venue coverage matter.

Risk / RewardPotential reward ÷ Potential loss

Compares a trade's planned upside with downside.

Drawdown from ATH((Current − ATH) ÷ ATH) × 100

Shows how far price is below its all-time high.

Break-even Sell PriceBuy price × (1 + fee rate) ÷ (1 − fee rate)

Approximation for matching buy and sell fees.

Important: No single formula can reliably label a cryptocurrency as genuine or a scam. Verify contracts, liquidity depth, ownership permissions, holder concentration, audits, exchange quality and independent sources. Security APIs can miss new or sophisticated attacks.
